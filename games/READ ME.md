# Game library

Open the site at `/calculator`. After unlocking the game catalog, open games at `/scientific-calculator`; select **Play Game** on any card. Minecraft 1.20.6 is available at `/graph-calculator`, with a link back to the catalog.

Subway Surfers includes eight city maps, available from its **Choose a city** card.

Some games stream their content and need an internet connection. Large games may take a little while to load.
