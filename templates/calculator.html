A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Frequently asked questions

Are these math tools free to use?

Yes. The calculator and study tools are free to use in your browser, with no account or download required.

Can I use these tools for homework?

They are handy for checking a result and reviewing a calculation. For homework, use them to support your understanding and show the steps your teacher asks for.

Are the numbers I enter saved?

Calculations happen in your browser. The site does not submit or save the values you enter; they stay in the page while you are using it.

Why might my answer look different from my worksheet?

Some problems require a particular number of decimal places, units, or an exact fraction. Check your class instructions and round only when asked.
